Z1 category artwork

The active My music tiles use artists-variant.png, songs-variant.png, albums-variant.png, playlists-variant.png and favorites-variant.png, generated with the built-in image_gen tool. The final prompts and reference mapping are in variants-prompts.json.

The original artists.png, songs.jpg, albums.jpg and playlists.jpg are retained as references. They come from the Android app opened by the user (com.sonyericsson.musicz1). Original Android resource names: portal_nav_item_artists, portal_nav_item_tracks, portal_nav_item_albums and portal_nav_item_playlists.
