Modern and Classic music previews

The category PNG assets were generated with the built-in image_gen tool using the connected Android My music screen as a style reference. The full prompts are in prompts.json.

These images are used only by Modern and Classic category cards and their Favorites submenu. Z1 keeps its separate images and layout.

Folders uses folders.png, generated with the built-in image_gen tool as a warm photograph of a wooden shelving unit full of vinyl record sleeves. It is shared by Modern, Classic and Z1. Its generation prompt is in folders-prompt.txt.
